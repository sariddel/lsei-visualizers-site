Defaults from the LSEI Scenario Trades workbook research base (driving_assumptions.json / commercial_break.json, verified 2026-07-13; CLPS re-verified against the Moon Base plan of record 2026-07-20): payloads are corporate capacity figures; first-landing years are the study's risk-adjusted judgments [J], not manifest dates (official targets are earlier: MK1 late 2026 → early 2027 after the New Glenn loss of 2026-05-28; Starship commercial cargo "2028" vs NASA OIG's ≥2-year HLS slip and the still-undemonstrated propellant transfer). Moon Base plan of record (nasa.gov/moonbase-phases, page updated 2026-06-11): Phase 1 to 2029 ≈ 4 t across 21 landings (~0.19 t avg); Phase 2 2029–32 up to 60 t across 24 landings (~2.5 t avg, "low, medium and heavy cargo-class landers" — classes not defined in kg anywhere public); Phase 3 2032+ up to 38 t/yr. The $590.4M CLPS awards of 2026-06-30 (Peregrine-2/-3, a Nova-C, a Blue Ghost variant, all late 2028) are all 100–150 kg class. Draper CP-12 was terminated Jul 2025. Cadence caps are study bounds: 2030 rail = 3–4 MK1 flights/yr; S1-2040 ≈ 7 MK2-class or ~2 Starship-class/yr; S3-2040 ≈ 10 Starship/yr; CLPS demonstrated max is 2 landings/yr (Isaacman aspiration: 10/yr from 2027). Each Starship landing additionally implies ~10–16 tanker launches to LEO — the cadence cap you set here is the constraint that number feeds. Scenario-state targets integrate the frozen Scenarios.docx annual-landed-mass rails (10→200/75/1,000 t/yr at 2030→2040) linearly to a 2026–2040 cumulative.
